About Vitreo Homeware

Founded in 2000, Vitreo Homeware has evolved from a local artisan workshop into a premium glassware manufacturer recognized for quality, innovation, and sustainability.

The company specializes in custom-made home-use glassware designed to transform everyday products into meaningful lifestyle experiences.

Its product portfolio includes drinking glasses, wine glasses, tea cups, coffee mugs, bowls, jars, decorative collections, personalized gifts, and corporate-branded glassware.

Sustainability remains a key priority through the use of recycled materials, recyclable packaging, and environmentally responsible production practices.
